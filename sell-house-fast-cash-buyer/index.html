Need a fast, private house sale?

A direct sale can help when a property is empty, inherited, dated, difficult to manage or poorly suited to months of public marketing. We review the property, make a written offer and purchase directly if the terms work for both sides.

This route is not automatically the best financial choice. An estate agent may achieve a higher headline price where the house is market-ready and the seller can accept viewings, a chain and a less certain timetable. We help you understand the trade-off before deciding.

Why sell directly instead of using an estate agent?

A direct sale removes the need to find a buyer through an open-market campaign. There are no estate-agent viewings or property chain, and the completion date can be arranged around access, probate or another practical requirement.

Direct sale may suit you if

  • Privacy and certainty matter
  • The house is empty or needs work
  • You want to avoid repeated viewings
  • A previous sale has fallen through
  • You need a flexible completion date

An estate agent may suit you if

  • Maximising the headline price is the priority
  • The house is presentable and mortgageable
  • You can wait through marketing and a chain
  • You are comfortable with public viewings
  • You can absorb preparation and holding costs

Take independent legal, valuation or tax advice where appropriate. We do not ask you to pretend a direct offer and an open-market sale are the same product.

Fast cash house sale FAQs

How quickly can you buy my house?+

Timing depends on the property, title, access and legal position. Where everything is ready, a direct purchase can avoid an estate-agent chain and repeated viewings. Completion can also be delayed to suit probate or your circumstances.

Will a direct cash offer equal the full open-market value?+

Usually not. Our offer reflects condition, works, resale costs and risk. In return, you may gain certainty, privacy, no estate-agent chain and less preparation. Compare the likely net proceeds and written terms of every route.

Do I need to repair or clear the house first?+

Not necessarily. We can consider empty, dated, inherited and probate-related properties with repairs or contents still inside. Any contents to remain should be agreed clearly before sale.

Are there estate agent fees?+

No estate agent is involved because we purchase directly. Review the written offer so you understand the price, assumptions and any costs before deciding.

Am I obligated after requesting an offer?+

No. The initial review and offer request are no obligation. You remain free to compare an estate agent, auction or another buyer and take independent advice.
