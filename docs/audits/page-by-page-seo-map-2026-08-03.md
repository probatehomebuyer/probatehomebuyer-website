## Intent ownership rules

- Commercial service pages own “sell”, “buyer”, “cash offer” and route-selection intent.
- Guides own evergreen processes and executor tasks.
- Articles own narrow questions, timely explanations and comparisons.
- FAQs provide short answers and links to the owner page.
- Location pages own a service-plus-place combination only when backed by unique local evidence.
